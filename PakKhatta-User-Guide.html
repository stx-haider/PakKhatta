1. What PakKhatta Does

PakKhatta is an offline desktop POS and business-management system. It records sales, purchases, stock, customers, suppliers, cash, bank accounts, expenses, returns, reports, and daily closing. Normal work does not require internet. Data is stored locally for the active business.

Important: Take regular database backups. Keep at least one backup on a USB drive or another computer.

2. First Launch and Secure Login

Create the owner account

  1. On first launch, enter the owner full name and Pakistani phone number.
  2. Complete phone OTP verification when required by the configured provider.
  3. Create the normal account password. It must meet the displayed strength rule.
  4. Set a separate System Function Password. It must contain 4 to 8 digits. This password is used to authorize Edit actions; it is not the normal login password.
  5. Create the account and sign in with the phone or username and normal password.

Staff accounts

Open Settings > Users and add a staff member with name, phone, login password, role, and a 4-to-8-digit System Function Password. Staff use their own credentials. Disable an account when the person leaves.

Edit protection

Every item, sale, and purchase Edit action opens a small password prompt. Enter the logged-in user’s System Function Password. A wrong or cancelled attempt makes no change. The normal password and function password are separate.

3. Switch Account and Business Privacy

  1. Click the business name in the top header to open Switch Account.
  2. The list shows only businesses linked to the currently logged-in account.
  3. Click a listed business to switch. Each business has its own customers, items, transactions, settings, and reports.
  4. Use Add New Business, enter the business name and optional phone/address, then choose Create & Switch.

The new business is linked to the active account. Other users on the same computer cannot see it unless it is explicitly linked to them. Close the popup with the close button, Cancel, or outside click.

4. Dashboard

The Dashboard is the daily overview for the active business. It shows today’s sales, returns, purchases, cash, bank balance, receivables, payables, stock value, expenses, low-stock alerts, expiry alerts, and recent transactions.

5. Items and Inventory

Create an item

  1. Open Items > Add Item.
  2. Enter the item name. Category, brand, unit, barcode, SKU, and location help searching and reporting.
  3. Enter purchase cost, retail sale price, wholesale price, MRP, tax, opening stock, and low-stock limit.
  4. Save the item. The system generates an item code when needed.

Stock increases through purchases and decreases through sales. Use Stock Adjustment for damage, expiry, loss, found stock, or a correction. The item history records the movement and reason. Average cost is maintained by the system.

Barcode: A USB scanner that types like a keyboard works with the sale and purchase barcode fields. Item code and SKU can also be used.

6. Purchases and the Item Dropdown

  1. Open Purchase > Add Purchase.
  2. Choose a supplier or the Walk-In Supplier option.
  3. Click + Add Item. The Item column is an inventory dropdown showing Item Name (Stock: X).
  4. Select an item. Its purchase cost fills the Price field automatically. Price remains editable.
  5. Enter quantity and expiry date if applicable. Row total is Quantity x Price and updates immediately.
  6. Add more rows, set discount/tax/paid amount, then save.

Purchase stock is added to inventory and cash, bank, or supplier payable is updated according to the payment. The system maintains average cost. Edit and delete actions are protected and recalculate related values.

7. Sales: Retail and Wholesale

  1. Open Sales > Add Sale.
  2. Choose Party Type: Customer or Supplier. The Party field starts with Walk-In Customer or Walk-In Supplier, followed by saved parties.
  3. Choose Pricing Mode: Retail uses Sale Price; Wholesale uses Wholesale Price.
  4. Search an item by name, item code, barcode, or SKU, or click + Add Item and select it.
  5. When an item is selected, its price fills automatically. You may edit the price. Change quantity as required; the row and grand totals update immediately.
  6. Set discount type and amount, tax, payment method, and paid amount.
  7. Choose Save Sale. Stock and cash/bank/receivable are updated and an invoice number is assigned.
ChoiceResult
Walk-In CustomerUse for a normal cash customer with no saved account. It is valid even when no customer record is selected.
Saved CustomerLinks the sale to that customer and updates the receivable when credit remains.
RetailUses the item’s standard Sale Price.
WholesaleUses the item’s Wholesale Price. If it is zero, review the item before selling.

The pricing mode changes existing rows in place. It does not reload the page or fetch the inventory again.

8. Returns and Corrections

Use Sales Return for goods returned by a customer and Purchase Return for goods sent back to a supplier. Select the original transaction, choose items and quantities, confirm the refund or adjustment method, and save. Stock, balances, cash/bank, sales, purchases, cost, and profit are reversed according to the return. Use Edit only when the original transaction itself needs correction.

9. Customers and Suppliers

  1. Open Parties and choose Customer or Supplier.
  2. Add the name, contact details, type, opening balance, and address.
  3. Open a party’s ledger to view transactions, payments, returns, and running balance.
  4. Use Receive Money for customer payments and Pay Supplier for supplier payments. Select Cash or Bank.

Opening balance represents an amount that existed before using PakKhatta. Do not delete a party with important linked transactions; edit the details instead.

10. Cash, Bank, and Expenses

Cash and bank

Cashbook records cash sales, customer receipts, supplier payments, expenses, refunds, and transfers. Add bank accounts with name and opening balance. Cash-to-bank and bank-to-cash transfers change the two balances only; they are not income, expense, sale, or profit.

Expenses

Open Expenses, enter category, amount, payment method, date, and note, then save. Cash expenses reduce cash; bank expenses reduce the selected bank balance. Expense reports group spending by date and category.

11. Reports and Daily Closing

Reports use date ranges and reflect the active business. Available reports include Sales, Purchases, Sales Returns, Purchase Returns, Profit & Loss, Stock, Low Stock, Expenses, Receivables/Payables, Ledgers, and Daily Summary. Print reports or use the browser print dialog to save PDF.

Close a day

  1. Open Settings > Day Closing and select the business date.
  2. Load the summary. Review cash, bank, credit, returns, purchases, expenses, and payments.
  3. Count the physical cash and enter Actual Counted Cash.
  4. Close the day. Expected Cash and Difference are saved for review.

12. Settings, Audit Log, and Backup

Business Profile controls the printed business name, phone, address, currency, tax registration, business type, and invoice footer. The Audit Log records who performed important actions and when.

Backup

  1. Use the database backup command in Settings and choose a safe location.
  2. Copy the backup to removable or external storage.
  3. To recover, select Restore Database. The application protects current data before loading the backup and may restart.
Do not delete or rename database files while PakKhatta is running. Close the application first and keep multiple dated backups.

13. Import, Export, Search, and Printing

  • Export item and party data for spreadsheet work and backup.
  • Import products using the expected spreadsheet/CSV columns. Test a small file first.
  • Use Global Search for items, invoices, customers, suppliers, phones, barcodes, and SKUs.
  • Invoices and reports can be printed or saved as PDF. Check printer settings and paper size before a busy sales period.

14. Staff Workflow and Quick Troubleshooting

Business missingConfirm that you are logged in to the account that owns it. Businesses are intentionally isolated.
